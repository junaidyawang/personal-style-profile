Before You Begin

Keep these simple guidelines in mind as you answer:

  • ✓
    There are no right or wrong answers.

    Every response helps build a clearer picture of your natural approach.

  • ✓
    Choose what feels more naturally like you.

    Avoid what you think your manager, mentor, or colleagues would prefer.

  • ✓
    Think about genuine choice.

    If both choices sound like you, pick the one that generally feels more comfortable, effortless, or instinctive.

  • ✓
    25 short questions.

    Questions 1–24 determine your style profile. Question 25 captures the single most useful thing for your mentor to know.

A Useful Conversation With Your Mentor

Use these exploratory prompts during your next 1-on-1 catch-up:

1

What helps you learn and contribute at your best? Reflect on situations where your natural style made work feel energized and effortless.

2

Where might your natural style be most useful in your role? Identify current projects where your perspective adds distinct value.

3

When might you need to flex to the opposite style? Notice scenarios where adapting your approach could yield better collaboration.

4

What could your mentor do that would help you grow? Agree on specific practices (e.g., meeting prep time, whiteboard brainstorming).
